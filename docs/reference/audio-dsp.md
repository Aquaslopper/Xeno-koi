# Audio effects

The three audio-effect controls are on **Options page 2**. They change match music when you capture cards or call Koi-Koi; they do not process menu music or card sound effects.

- **Chorus** adds a short, gently modulated layer to the music.
- **Bass boost** increases the low-frequency weight of the music.
- **Space reverb** adds room-like reflections, from a smaller space toward a larger, longer-sounding room.

The effect builds after a capture or Koi-Koi call, then fades. The sliders set its strength. Fresh settings start with Chorus off, Bass boost at **+0 dB**, and Space reverb at **50%**.

Use **TEST EFFECT: ON/OFF** beneath the sliders to compare the selected match music with the effects enabled and bypassed. Changes to the sliders take effect during the audition. The test is temporary and is not saved; leaving Options page 2 stops it. The browser version uses its bundled music for the audition.

Press **BACK (SAVE)** to store your effect settings. The same controls work in the browser and desktop versions.
