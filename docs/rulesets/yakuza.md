# Yakuza (Oya-ken)

**Tournament scoring plus Dealer's Privilege.** Card scoring is exactly the same as Tournament. The only addition is that a round that ends with no winner pays the dealer.

## Summary

- **Scoring is identical to Tournament:** Viewings are disabled, and the Sake Cup is an animal.
- **Dealer's Privilege:** when a round ends in a draw, the current dealer wins and receives **6 points**.
- A round is a draw in two cases: **exhaustion** (both hands empty, nobody called Shobu), and **both players lucky**.
- The dealer keeps the deal after a Dealer's Privilege round.
- **One Koi-Koi call per player per round**, then a forced Shobu, as in Nintendo and Tournament.

## Dealer's Privilege in detail

| Situation | Normal rule sets | Yakuza |
|---|---|---|
| Exhaustion (no winner) | 0–0 draw, dealer stays | **Dealer wins 6 points**, dealer stays |
| Both players lucky | 0–0 draw | **Dealer wins 6 points** |
| One player lucky | That player wins 6 | That player wins 6 (unchanged) |

Dealer's Privilege is a flat 6 points. It is not multiplied by Koi-Koi calls or the 7+ rule.

This is a documented rule, not an invention: the traditional **Oya-ken** (親権, "Dealer's Privilege", also *oya gachi*). Japanese Koi-Koi guides pay the dealer exactly 6 points when both hands empty with no yaku, and list it beside the lucky hands as a flat rule:

> 「どちらも『勝負』をせず、札がなくなって流れた場合は『親権』として**親に6点**が入ります」 — [rinchar](https://rinchar.site/hanahuda-koikoi/)

> "**Oya-Ken (Dealer's Privilege)** — If neither gets any Yaku, Dealer wins 6 points." — [gamedesign.jp](https://www.gamedesign.jp/flash/hanafuda/rule_e.html)

The same lists carry its mirror, **Tsukazu** (6 points to the *non-dealer* instead). Two places where Yakuza goes further than the sources: traditional Oya-ken covers the exhaustion draw only — extending it to the both-lucky draw is this rule set's choice — and a few sources pay the dealer 1 point rather than 6.

## Why the dealer matters

Because a draw pays the dealer and the dealer keeps the deal, being the dealer is worth more on Yakuza than on the other rule sets. A dealer can collect 6 points from a draw and still be dealer for the next round.
