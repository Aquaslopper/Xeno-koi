## 1. How the Story Forms & Triggers

The narrative of **ATRIUM PLEX** is not delivered as a conventional linear cutscene or wall of exposition. Instead, it unfolds as **weird, surreal, non-linear art and transmissions** tied directly to the player's entry and monthly progression through the 12-Month / 48-Card tree:

1. **First Entry into the Atrium Plex (`Introduction — Sequence 00`):**
   - The very first time the player clicks **`ATRIUM PLEX`** and crosses the loading transition into `assets/plexus.png`, the **Introduction** plays automatically.
   - This sets the atmosphere of the Plexus — the silent biomechanical profile, the synaptic spire, and the sensation of stepping out of the tournament hall into a strange inner conditioning space.
2. **Monthly Continuations (`Months 01–12`):**
   - Each of the **12 Months** holds its own chapter/fragment of the story.
   - Unlocking or entering a new Month triggers the next continuation — layering surreal imagery, cryptic visual fragments, and non-linear lore as the player ascends deeper into the deck.
3. **Re-Viewing / Archive:**
   - Any story fragment already witnessed can be revisited inside the Plexus so the player can piece together the non-linear fragments at their own pace.

---

## 2. Tone & Artistic Direction

- **Weird, Biomechanical, Non-Linear Art:**
  - Rather than explaining everything literally, each monthly sequence behaves like a feverish biomechanical vision or neural imprint — fragmented, atmospheric, and open to interpretation.
  - Visuals and text fragments can shift order, echo specific suits/months of the Hanafuda calendar, or react to how far the player's conditioning has progressed.
- **Open-Ended Evolution:**
  - The exact scenes, artwork, and text for the Introduction and each of the 12 Months will take form organically during brainstorming and art creation.
